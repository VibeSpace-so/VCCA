# Marketing playbook

## When this becomes relevant

- Building a landing page.
- Planning a launch.
- Choosing distribution channels.
- Creating content, ads, or partnerships.
- Improving growth, SEO, or referral.

## Questions to ask

- Who is the exact audience, and where do they already spend time?
- What is the one-sentence value proposition?
- What is the channel's unit economics (CAC vs LTV)?
- What is the repeatable motion, not just the one-off launch?
- What does the conversion funnel look like?
- Can you reach 100 people without paid spend?
- What is the story that will make someone share?

## Common anti-patterns

- Building the product before building a landing page.
- Treating launch day as the end of marketing.
- Spray-and-pray across every channel.
- No tracking or attribution.
- No landing page before writing code.
- Ignoring organic reach and relying only on ads.
- Marketing to everyone instead of one narrow segment.

## Decision heuristics

- Start with channels you can access for free or cheap.
- Build a landing page before the product is finished.
- Measure CAC and LTV per channel.
- Launch is a continuous process, not a single day.
- Create content for the audience's jobs-to-be-done.
- Focus on one channel until it plateaus.

## Teaching moments

- "How do I get my first users?" → Go where they already gather.
- "When should I launch?" → Launch continuously; launch day is a milestone, not the start.
- "Should I run ads?" → Only after organic channels show repeatable CAC.
